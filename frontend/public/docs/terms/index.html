What Raven is

Raven is a tool for testing the resilience of AI chatbot system prompts against known adversarial attack patterns. It is provided as-is for educational and security research purposes. It is not a certified security auditing service, and a passing scan result does not constitute a professional security certification or guarantee.

Acceptable use

You may use Raven to test system prompts that you own or have explicit permission to test. You may not use Raven to:

  • Test system prompts belonging to third parties without their knowledge and consent.
  • Attempt to reverse-engineer, extract, or reproduce the system prompts of third-party deployed chatbots by running them through Raven.
  • Circumvent or attempt to circumvent rate limits through automated requests, multiple accounts, or other means.
  • Use scan results to cause harm to others or to assist in attacks against AI systems.
  • Submit prompts that contain illegal content, personally identifiable information belonging to others, or content that violates applicable laws.

Scan results and accuracy

Raven's scan results are informational. They reflect the behaviour of the specific sandboxed model instance used at the time of the scan. A system prompt that passes all 25 attacks in Raven may still be vulnerable to attacks not in Raven's library, to attacks specific to your deployment platform, or to attacks that work differently against your production model. Raven does not replace a full security review.

Raven tests your system prompt in isolation. It does not account for your deployment platform's own safeguards, your production model's specific behaviour, or runtime context that may affect security in your live deployment.
